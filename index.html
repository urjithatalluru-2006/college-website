About Our College

Education that empowers. Values that inspire.

Malineni Women's College is committed to creating an inspiring educational environment where students can discover their potential, develop professional skills and become responsible contributors to society.

Our academic environment combines classroom learning with practical exposure, extracurricular activities, technology and opportunities for personal development.

  • Student-focused learning environment
